Founded in 1996 by Ian Davies and Graham Bonnick, Anchor Flooring Ltd is a contract and commercial flooring company based in Lancaster.

From carpets and vinyl to the finishing details, we offer a wide choice of flooring products. The same practical approach runs through everything we do: the right floor, carefully fitted.
