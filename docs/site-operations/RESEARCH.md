## Method and limits

A focused UK-oriented search sample used the queries below through the available web search tool, followed by inspection of relevant first-party pages. Returned order was **not recorded as Google ranking**: the tool is not a controlled Google UK SERP, its location/device are not fixed and results can change. Presence means the page was returned in this research sample. Competitor claims are their own statements, not independently verified achievements. Search volume, difficulty, organic traffic, backlink counts, Anthēon's live positions and historic rankings are **unavailable**. No paid data source, unauthorised Google scraper or Search Console account was used.

Sample queries:

- `web design Buckinghamshire agency websites`
- `website redesign UK agency service`
- `web design construction companies UK specialists websites`
- `SEO monitoring optimisation small business UK monthly service`
- `website maintenance management hosting small business UK services`

## Search-console and search-feature evidence

- Prefer an owner-controlled **Domain property `antheon.co.uk`**, represented by the API as `sc-domain:antheon.co.uk`. The owner must obtain the exact verification value from Search Console and approve any new DNS record. Add it alongside existing records, retain it after verification, and do not change nameservers. [Google property setup](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/34592?hl=en) · [Ownership verification](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/9008080?hl=en)
- Canonical metadata, internal links and sitemap entries should consistently use the verified HTTPS www origin. Canonicals are signals; Google can select another URL. [Canonical guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/consolidate-duplicate-urls)
- Generating a sitemap, submitting it, Google processing it and individual pages being indexed are separate states. Sitemap availability does not guarantee indexing or ranking. [Google crawling/indexing FAQ](https://developers.google.com/search/help/crawling-index-faq)
- The URL Inspection API reports the indexed version; it does not perform a live indexability test or force indexing. A manual Search Console live test/indexing request is a separate owner action. [API method](https://developers.google.com/webmaster-tools/v1/urlInspection.index/inspect)
- Google's May 2026 changelog records FAQ rich results ending from **7 May 2026**. Keep useful visitor FAQs, but do not present FAQ schema as a rich-result opportunity. [Google documentation updates](https://developers.google.com/search/updates?hl=en)

No Search Console ownership, sitemap submission, page indexing, GA4 data flow, key-event setup, continuous monitor or authority submission was verified in this research. Local implementation/testing status belongs in the implementation report and requirements matrix.
